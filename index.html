Selected work

Five problems, handled.

Each one started with a team losing time or money. The setting was enterprise support; the approach works anywhere.

CASE-01 Adopted

A two-month change process, cut to two weeks

Problem Every workflow change went through a hand-off chain of around ten people. Changes took two months on average, and some took six.
What I did Mapped the chain, removed the hand-offs that added no value, and made changes directly where I could, updating 11 workflows myself.

Average turnaround down from 2 months to 2 weeks, some same-day. Named a top-5 accomplishment for the organisation.

CASE-02 Shipped

Shipping an AI tool without an engineering team

Problem Hundreds of project ideas a year arrived through an unstructured process, with duplicate submissions and slow routing.
What I did Designed and built a full-stack AI application with no engineering support, using LLM-driven semantic search for duplicate detection, AI-generated one-pagers and automatic routing.

The standard intake tool for 300+ people, handling 400+ submissions a year, and the only perfect 10/10 at the organisation's hackathon.

CASE-03 Adopted

A $4.1M case for self-service

Problem A new self-service portal needed a structure customers could find their way around, or simple requests would keep landing with the support team.
What I did Kicked off and led the team that designed the portal's customer-facing taxonomy, validated it with UX research, and measured the result after launch.

Self-service deflection up 32.4% and cases per employee down 21.3%, a projected $4.1M a year in efficiency gains.

CASE-04 Delivered

Turning a noisy backlog into shipped change

Problem Without someone validating impact and prioritising against engineering capacity, an improvement backlog turns into noise instead of progress.
What I did Owned the backlog and bi-weekly sprints with in-house and external engineers: validated the business case for each request, decided what to build next, and ran testing and release with stakeholders.

80 changes and fixes shipped to production in six months, including a usability redesign that saved ~860 hours a year.

CASE-05 In rollout

An AI roadmap for a 300-person organisation

Problem The organisation's core platform had real potential to cut manual work with AI, but no defined roadmap for getting there.
What I did Defined requirements for 10 AI features, including case classification, summarisation and workflow automation, working directly with an external technology vendor on design and rollout planning.
